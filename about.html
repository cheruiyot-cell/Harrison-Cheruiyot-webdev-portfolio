My Story & Mission

I started building websites in Nairobi because I saw a gap: too many brilliant Kenyan business owners – from gym owners to restaurant managers to logistics companies – were losing opportunities because they had no digital presence or their website was slow, confusing, and invisible on Google.

My mission is simple: help Kenyan SMEs get a website that works as hard as they do. That means:

  • Fast, mobile-first design that loads even on slow networks
  • Built-in M‑Pesa payments so customers can pay in seconds
  • SEO‑friendly structure so Google finds you and sends you customers
  • Honest, jargon‑free communication – you'll always know what's happening

My promise: If your website doesn't bring measurable results within 30 days, I'll work for free to improve it. Your success is my reputation.

Why I'm Different

I'm not a big agency that treats you like a number. When you work with me, you work directly with me – the developer, the designer, the strategist. I personally handle every project from the first message to launch and beyond.

I've also lived in Kenya my whole life, so I understand the local context: the importance of M‑Pesa, the challenges of internet reliability, and what Kenyan customers actually look for when they visit a website.
