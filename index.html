Your Questions, Answered Honestly

Honest answers to the questions I hear most often.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites take 3–7 days from our first call to launch. The exact timeline depends on how prepared you are with your content — but I'll give you a clear time frame during our first call, and I'll stick to it.

I don't know what I want. Can you help?

Absolutely. Most of my clients don't know exactly what their website needs. I'll ask you simple questions about your business, your customers, and your goals. Then I'll suggest exactly what you need — no pushy upsells, just practical advice.

Why should I pay for a custom site when I can use Wix?

Wix sites are slow, hard to customize, and they rank poorly on Google. Your website is your 24/7 salesperson — you don't want it sleeping on the job. I build custom, fast websites that actually get found on Google and convert visitors into customers. A custom site pays for itself by bringing in more business.

How do you handle M-Pesa payments?

I integrate the official Lipa Na M-Pesa gateway. Your customer gets a pop-up on their phone, enters their M-Pesa PIN, and the payment completes in about 10 seconds. They never leave your website. You get notified instantly via SMS and in your dashboard.

What happens after the website goes live?

Every project includes 30 days of free support. That means if something breaks, or you want a small change like updating your price list, I fix it within 24 hours — at no extra charge. After 30 days, you can choose a maintenance plan (from Ksh 2,500/month) or just contact me when you need changes.

Do you only work with businesses in Nairobi?

Not at all. I work with businesses all over Kenya — Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, and everywhere in between. We do everything through calls, WhatsApp, and screen sharing. You don't need to be in Nairobi to get a world-class website.

How do I know you're legit?

Before you pay anything, you'll see live websites I've built for Kenyan businesses — you can click through them right now. I'll also give you contact details of past clients if you want to verify my work. Payment is split: 50% upfront to start, 50% only when you're happy with the final website.

What if I need changes after the 30 days?

After your 30-day support period, you can choose a maintenance plan starting at Ksh 2,500/month. This includes security updates, hosting, and small content changes. If you'd rather not commit, I can also do one-off changes for a small fee. You'll never be left stranded.

What if your website doesn't bring me customers?

I build every website with conversion in mind — not just good looks. If your website doesn't generate measurable results within 30 days, I'll work with you for free to improve it. Your success is my reputation — I want you to refer me to other business owners.
