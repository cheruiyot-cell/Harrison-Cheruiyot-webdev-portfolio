Common Questions (and Direct Answers)

I've addressed the questions I hear most often from business owners in Nairobi. If you don't see yours, just reach out.

How long will it take to build my business website?

A typical business growth website takes about 3 to 5 weeks from our first chat to launch. The exact timeline depends on how complex your site is and how quickly we can gather all the content, like photos and text. I'll give you a clear, realistic timeline during our first meeting.

Do you only work with businesses in Nairobi?

Not at all! While I'm based in Nairobi, I work remotely with ambitious SMEs across Kenya. Whether you're in Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, or Nakuru, our process of calls, WhatsApp chats, and screen-sharing makes the distance feel non-existent. The final product will always feel local to you.

How do you handle M-Pesa payments on my website?

I integrate the official, secure Lipa Na M-Pesa payment gateway directly into your site. This provides a pop-up on your phone for payment or a simple, automated USSD push for a seamless checkout. Your customers never leave your website, and the payment reflects instantly in your till.

Can you redesign my old, slow website?

Absolutely, and this is a very common request. I can carefully rebuild your existing site with clean, modern code to make it faster, secure, and beautiful on mobile—all while preserving the content and search engine ranking you've already built up. Think of it as giving your digital shop a major renovation.

What happens after the website is launched? Will I be left on my own?

Never. Every project comes with 30 days of post-launch support for any questions or tiny fixes. After that, you can opt for one of my affordable monthly maintenance plans where I handle updates, backups, and security, so you can focus entirely on your business without a single tech worry.

Do I need to buy a domain name and hosting myself?

Don't worry about the technical stuff. I will guide you through buying the perfect domain name (like www.yourbusiness.co.ke) and set up fast, reliable hosting on your behalf. I'll handle all the configuration, so it's one less thing on your plate. I just work with your brand name and leave you with all the account details.
