Common Questions

Honest answers to the questions I hear most often.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites take 3 to 5 weeks from our first call to launch. The exact timeline depends on how complex your site is and how quickly we get all the content. I'll give you a clear timeline during our first meeting.

I don't know what I want. Can you help?

Yes, I guide you through everything. Most business owners know they need a website but don't know what it should have. I'll make suggestions based on your business type and goals, so you don't have to figure it out alone.

Why should I pay for a custom site when I can use Wix?

Templates are fine for personal blogs, but for a business, they are slow and hard to customize. My custom code is built for speed and to rank on Google. A custom site pays for itself by bringing in more customers.

How do you handle M-Pesa payments?

I integrate the official Lipa Na M-Pesa gateway into your site. Customers get a simple pop-up on their phone to complete payment quickly and securely. They never leave your website.

What happens after the website goes live?

Every project includes 30 days of free support for any questions or small fixes. After that, you can choose a maintenance plan where I handle updates and security so you don't have to worry about it.

Do you only work with businesses in Nairobi?

Not at all. I work with businesses all over Kenya — Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, wherever you are. We can do everything through calls, WhatsApp, and screen sharing.
